Our story

About Us

Built on passion, served with love — every detail at The Tea Stage is crafted with intention.

Est. 2020, Chitwan

Where Every Cup Tells a Story

The Tea Stage was born in 2020 from a simple yet powerful vision: to create a café experience that goes beyond just food and drinks. Nestled in the heart of Chitwan, we set out to build a sanctuary — a place where every detail, from the aroma of freshly brewed masala chai to the warm glow of our lighting, is crafted with intention.

Our founders believed that a great café isn't just about what's on the menu — it's about the feeling you carry with you when you leave. That feeling of warmth, of belonging, of having experienced something genuinely special.

Today, The Tea Stage is Chitwan's most loved premium café, welcoming thousands of guests each month and building memories one cup at a time.
